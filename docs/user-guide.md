# Student User Guide

Welcome to Psynergy! This comprehensive guide will help you navigate the platform and make the most of its mental health support features.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. **Visit the Registration Page**
   - Navigate to the Psynergy homepage
   - Click "Create Account" to begin registration

2. **Fill in Your Information**
   - **Full Name**: Enter your complete name as it appears in college records
   - **Email**: Use your college email or personal email address
   - **USN**: Enter your University Seat Number (e.g., 1RV21CS099)
   - **College**: Search and select your college from the dropdown
   - **Year of Study**: Select your current academic year
   - **Language**: Choose your preferred language from 13+ options

3. **Optional Information**
   - **Phone Number**: Indian mobile number for notifications
   - **Gender**: Select your gender identity (inclusive options available)
   - **Pronouns**: Choose your preferred pronouns

4. **Complete Registration**
   - Accept the terms and conditions
   - Click "Create Account"
   - Verify your email if required

### First Login

After registration, you'll be taken to your personalized dashboard where you can:
- Complete your profile
- Take an initial mental health assessment
- Explore available resources
- Start chatting with the AI counselor

## 🤖 AI Mental Health Support

### Starting a Conversation

1. **Access the Chat**
   - Click "Chat with AI Counselor" from your dashboard
   - Or navigate to the Chat section from the main menu

2. **Begin Your Conversation**
   - Type your message in the chat box
   - Press Enter or click Send
   - The AI will respond with supportive guidance

### What You Can Discuss

- **Academic Stress**: Exam anxiety, study pressure, performance concerns
- **Personal Issues**: Relationship problems, family stress, identity questions
- **Mental Health**: Depression, anxiety, mood changes, sleep issues
- **Crisis Situations**: Immediate support and emergency resources
- **General Wellness**: Self-care tips, coping strategies, lifestyle advice

### Crisis Detection

The AI automatically monitors conversations for crisis indicators:
- **High-Risk Situations**: Immediate emergency resources provided
- **Moderate Risk**: Counselor referrals and additional support
- **Low Risk**: Continued supportive conversation and resources

### Language Support

- Switch languages anytime during conversation
- All 13+ Indian regional languages supported
- Cultural context considered in responses

## 📊 Mental Health Assessments

### Available Assessments

1. **PHQ-9 (Depression Assessment)**
   - 9 questions about mood and interest
   - Takes 3-5 minutes to complete
   - Provides depression severity score

2. **GAD-7 (Anxiety Assessment)**
   - 7 questions about anxiety symptoms
   - Quick 2-3 minute assessment
   - Measures anxiety levels

3. **Student Stress Assessment**
   - College-specific stress evaluation
   - Covers academic and personal stressors
   - Tailored recommendations provided

### Taking an Assessment

1. **Access Assessments**
   - Click "Take Assessment" from dashboard
   - Or access through the Chat interface
   - Select assessment type

2. **Complete Questions**
   - Answer honestly for accurate results
   - Use "Previous" and "Next" to navigate
   - All responses are confidential

3. **Review Results**
   - Receive immediate scoring and interpretation
   - Get personalized recommendations
   - Option to share results with counselor

### Understanding Your Results

- **Minimal/None**: Low concern, continue self-care
- **Mild**: Some symptoms present, consider resources
- **Moderate**: Noticeable impact, counseling recommended
- **Severe**: Significant symptoms, immediate support needed

## 📅 Counseling Services

### Booking an Appointment

1. **Browse Counselors**
   - View available counselors and specializations
   - Check ratings, experience, and languages
   - Read counselor profiles and approaches

2. **Select Date and Time**
   - Choose from available slots up to 14 days ahead
   - Select preferred time (morning, afternoon, evening)
   - Consider your schedule and preferences

3. **Choose Session Type**
   - **Individual Session**: One-on-one counseling
   - **Group Session**: Small group support
   - **Crisis Session**: Emergency support (priority booking)

4. **Privacy Options**
   - **Identified Session**: Use your real name
   - **Anonymous Session**: Complete privacy protection
   - **Partial Anonymity**: Limited information sharing

### Preparing for Your Session

- **Before the Appointment**
  - Complete any recommended assessments
  - Prepare topics you want to discuss
  - Ensure stable internet connection
  - Find a private, comfortable space

- **During the Session**
  - Be honest and open with your counselor
  - Ask questions about coping strategies
  - Take notes if helpful
  - Discuss follow-up plans

- **After the Session**
  - Review any homework or exercises given
  - Schedule follow-up if recommended
  - Practice suggested coping techniques
  - Reach out if you need immediate support

### Emergency Appointments

For crisis situations:
- **Immediate Booking**: Priority access to next available slot
- **24/7 Support**: Emergency counselors available
- **Crisis Resources**: Immediate helpline numbers provided
- **Safety Planning**: Collaborative safety plan development
