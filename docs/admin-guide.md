# Administrator Guide

This guide provides comprehensive instructions for college administrators using the Psynergy administrative dashboard to monitor student mental health trends and manage the platform.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Admin Account Setup

1. **Account Creation**
   - Contact Psynergy support for admin account creation
   - Provide college verification documents
   - Receive admin credentials and setup instructions

2. **Initial Configuration**
   - Set up college-specific settings
   - Configure crisis alert thresholds
   - Customize notification preferences
   - Set up counselor accounts

3. **Dashboard Access**
   - Navigate to `/admin` on your Psynergy instance
   - Log in with admin credentials
   - Complete initial setup wizard

### Admin Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides real-time insights into:
- **Student Engagement**: Platform usage and participation
- **Mental Health Trends**: Aggregate assessment data
- **Crisis Alerts**: High-risk situations requiring attention
- **Resource Utilization**: Most accessed content and services
- **Counselor Performance**: Session metrics and availability

## 🚨 Crisis Alert Management

### Alert Types and Severity Levels

#### **Critical Alerts (Red)**
- **Immediate Risk**: Suicidal ideation or self-harm indicators
- **Crisis Keywords**: Detected in chat or forum posts
- **Assessment Scores**: Severe depression or anxiety levels
- **Emergency Situations**: Requires immediate intervention

#### **High Priority Alerts (Orange)**
- **Moderate Risk**: Concerning assessment results
- **Behavioral Changes**: Significant platform usage changes
- **Multiple Indicators**: Combination of risk factors
- **Follow-up Required**: Professional assessment needed

#### **Medium Priority Alerts (Yellow)**
- **Mild Concerns**: Elevated stress or anxiety levels
- **Academic Stress**: Exam-related mental health impacts
- **Social Issues**: Relationship or family problems
- **Monitoring Needed**: Regular check-ins recommended

#### **Low Priority Alerts (Green)**
- **General Wellness**: Routine mental health check-ins
- **Resource Requests**: Students seeking information
- **Positive Indicators**: Improvement in mental health metrics
- **Preventive Care**: Proactive support opportunities

### Alert Response Protocols

1. **Immediate Response (Critical Alerts)**
   - **Automatic Notification**: Instant alerts to crisis team
   - **Emergency Contacts**: Provide immediate helpline numbers
   - **Counselor Assignment**: Priority booking with crisis counselor
   - **Safety Planning**: Collaborative safety plan development
   - **Follow-up Tracking**: Monitor student until resolution

2. **Timely Response (High Priority)**
   - **24-Hour Response**: Contact student within one day
   - **Counselor Referral**: Schedule appointment within 48 hours
   - **Resource Provision**: Relevant mental health resources
   - **Progress Monitoring**: Regular check-ins and follow-up

3. **Scheduled Response (Medium/Low Priority)**
   - **Weekly Review**: Include in regular case management
   - **Resource Recommendations**: Suggest appropriate content
   - **Group Support**: Invite to relevant support groups
   - **Preventive Interventions**: Proactive wellness programs

### Crisis Team Coordination

#### **Team Roles**
- **Crisis Coordinator**: Overall crisis response management
- **Mental Health Counselors**: Direct student intervention
- **Academic Advisors**: Academic support and accommodations
- **Campus Security**: Safety and emergency response
- **Medical Staff**: Physical health and psychiatric referrals

#### **Communication Protocols**
- **Alert Notifications**: Immediate team notifications
- **Case Conferences**: Regular team meetings for complex cases
- **Documentation**: Detailed case notes and intervention records
- **Escalation Procedures**: When to involve external services

## 🆘 Emergency Procedures

### Crisis Response

1. **Immediate Actions**
   - Assess severity and immediate risk
   - Contact emergency services if necessary
   - Notify crisis team and relevant personnel
   - Document all actions and communications

2. **Student Safety**
   - Ensure immediate safety and support
   - Coordinate with campus security
   - Involve family or emergency contacts if appropriate
   - Provide ongoing monitoring and follow-up

3. **System Response**
   - Update alert status and resolution
   - Document lessons learned
   - Review and improve response procedures
   - Provide support to staff involved

### Technical Emergencies

1. **System Outages**
   - Activate backup communication channels
   - Provide alternative crisis resources
   - Communicate with users about service status
   - Coordinate with technical support team

2. **Data Breaches**
   - Immediately secure affected systems
   - Assess scope and impact of breach
   - Notify affected users and authorities
   - Implement remediation measures

3. **Recovery Procedures**
   - Restore services as quickly as possible
   - Verify data integrity and security
   - Communicate resolution to stakeholders
   - Conduct post-incident review and improvement

Remember: The administrative dashboard is a powerful tool for supporting student mental health. Use it responsibly, maintain student privacy, and always prioritize student safety and wellbeing in your decisions and actions.
